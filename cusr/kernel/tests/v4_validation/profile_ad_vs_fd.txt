Takeaways:
  - AD forward-mode J production is 3.0x faster than FD's K-redundant differencing (88.7 -> 29.7 ms)
    — the direct mechanism behind v4's speedup.
  - J production drops from 67.5% of FD's loop to 38.6% of AD's loop. The two tree-interpreter passes
    (ad_jacobian 29.7 + eval 27.5 = 57.2 ms) now jointly dominate (74% of loop) -> confirms v4.1
    (fuse the value/residual eval into the AD pass) as the next lever.

    2431 (AD) vs 1776 (FD) = 1.37x. Loop-only is 1.70x; gap widens with M as setup amortizes
