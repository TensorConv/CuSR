CuSR baseline: Operon LMOptimizer vs the GPU kernel (FD & AD) on identical pre-CO trees

2026-06-17

问题 / Question. 在完全相同的 pre-CO 树上,我们的 GPU 常数优化核(FD 与 AD 两版)相比成熟 CPU 优化器 Operon LMOptimizer,收敛性与最终拟合质量如何?全部 17 题,看能发现什么。On byte-identical pre-CO trees, how do our GPU constant-optimization kernels (FD and AD) compare to a mature CPU optimizer (Operon's LMOptimizer) in convergence and final fit quality? All 17 problems — exploratory.

实验设置 / Setup. 每题(cap=32, seed=0, noise=0)用 operon_dump.run_cell 确定性重生成 Operon 语料中完全相同的 pre-CO 种群(逐字节 == 已提交语料,Probe B)。每个 checkpoint 代:(1) Operon 自带 CoefficientOptimizer(LMOptimizer) 从相同 c_init 优化每棵树;(2) 核 FD(batch_lm_fusedfd)与 AD(batch_lm_ad)跑同一批 pop.bin。三者同起点(Probe A:相同起点/相同目标 0.5·SSE/相同 K/确定性);最终用中立 fp64 0.5·Σ(f-y)² 对每个引擎返回的系数统一重算 —— 与引擎自报代价无关。Per problem (cap=32, seed=0, noise=0) we deterministically regenerate the EXACT Operon pre-CO population via operon_dump.run_cell (byte-identical to the committed corpus — Probe B). Per checkpoint gen: (1) Operon's own CoefficientOptimizer(LMOptimizer) optimizes every tree from the harvested c_init; (2) kernel FD (batch_lm_fusedfd) and AD (batch_lm_ad) run on the same pop.bin. All three start from the same c_init (Probe A: same start / same 0.5·SSE objective / same K / deterministic). Each engine's returned coefficients are scored by the NEUTRAL fp64 0.5·Σ(f-y)².

发现 / Findings

【受控·主结果】用本实验自己的金标准(中立 fp64 >1% 显著改进)衡量,AD 比 FD 净多改进 6959 棵树(AD 324082 vs FD 317123 / 340000)。机制:AD 把 46574 棵 FD-Cholesky-失败救成收敛(Cholesky AD 32036 vs FD 77517),代价是新增 17604 棵 FD-收敛→AD-未收敛。[CONTROLLED · headline] By the experiment's OWN gold standard (neutral fp64 >1% improvement), AD genuinely improves 6959 more trees than FD (AD 324082 vs FD 317123 of 340000). Mechanism: AD rescues 46574 FD-Cholesky-fails to convergence (Cholesky AD 32036 vs FD 77517), at the cost of 17604 new FD-converged→AD-not-converged.
证据 / evidence: 中立净改进 +6959;状态迁移(机制,非头条):4→0=46574, 4→4=22894, 0→4=8069, 0→2=6214。状态收敛差 +42344 比中立净改进高 ~6.1×(status≠跨引擎可比,见注),故以中立净改进为准。Neutral net +6959; status migration (mechanism, not headline): 4→0=46574, 4→4=22894, 0→4=8069, 0→2=6214. The raw status-conv gap +42344 is ~6.1× the neutral net (status is not cross-comparable; see caveats), so we lead with the neutral net.
【受控】无导数 bug:FD 与 AD 都显著改进的 312382 棵树上,最终 0.5·SSE 几乎一致(log10(AD/FD) 中位 -0.0;AD 更优 71097, FD 更优 72615);且该近似打平在 17 题中每一题内部都成立(非正负抵消)。[CONTROLLED] No derivative bug: on 312382 trees where both FD and AD genuinely improve, final 0.5·SSE is essentially identical (median log10(AD/FD)=-0.0; AD-better 71097, FD-better 72615) — and the near-tie holds WITHIN each of the 17 problems (per-problem median |log10(AD/FD)| ≤ 0.0).
证据 / evidence: 共同显著改进集质量比 p10 -0.4305, p90 0.3395;每题中位 log10(AD/FD) 绝对值 ≤ 0.0(17 题全部,见 adfd_quality_per_problem)。common-genuinely-improved quality ratio p10 -0.4305, p90 0.3395; per-problem median |log10(AD/FD)| ≤ 0.0 on all 17 (adfd_quality_per_problem).
【同口径效能·按题异质】合并中位损失比给出“Operon 在 bloated 树上更深”的印象,但这是少数高 K 题主导的量级效应,**按题排序会翻转**:g100 每题中位比,Operon 更优 7 题、核(FD)更优 5 题、打平 5 题;对 AD 则 Operon 6、AD 7、打平 4。没有哪道题长得像合并三元组。[efficacy · per-problem heterogeneity] The pooled median loss-ratio suggests 'Operon goes deeper on bloated trees', but that is a magnitude effect dominated by a few high-K problems — the per-problem ordering FLIPS. At g100, Operon's median is better on 7 problems, the kernel (FD) on 5, tied on 5; for AD it is Operon 6 / AD 7 / tie 4. No single problem looks like the pooled triple.
证据 / evidence: 合并各代中位损失比 — Operon: g0: 0.4465; g4: 0.0671; g16: 0.0016; g64: 0.0014; g100: 0.0035; FD: g0: 0.4142; g4: 0.081; g16: 0.003; g64: 0.0063; g100: 0.0097; AD: g0: 0.4046; g4: 0.0801; g16: 0.0031; g64: 0.0043; g100: 0.0062。按题表见下;>2% 差距才算一题之胜负。pooled median loss-ratio by gen — Operon: g0: 0.4465; g4: 0.0671; g16: 0.0016; g64: 0.0014; g100: 0.0035; FD: g0: 0.4142; g4: 0.081; g16: 0.003; g64: 0.0063; g100: 0.0097; AD: g0: 0.4046; g4: 0.0801; g16: 0.0031; g64: 0.0043; g100: 0.0062. See the per-problem table; >2% apart = a per-problem win.
【外部标尺·按题异质】“稳健性差距随 bloat 增大”只对少数高 K 题成立:17 题中 6 题g0→g100 差距扩大、11 题反而缩小。合并曲线的扩大主要由这几题驱动,不是普遍现象。[yardstick · per-problem heterogeneity] 'The robustness gap widens with bloat' holds only for a minority: of 17 problems, 6 widen g0→g100 while 11 NARROW. The pooled trend is driven by those few high-K problems, not a general phenomenon.
证据 / evidence: 按题 g0→g100 差距(核未能 >1% 改进 Operon 已改进的树之比例)见 report_aggregates.json robustness_gap_widen_narrow.per_problem。Per-problem g0→g100 gap (fraction of Operon->improved trees the kernel did not improve >1%) in report_aggregates.json robustness_gap_widen_narrow.per_problem.
【fp32 诚实性·严抓项】核以 fast-math fp32 优化,少数树**自报收敛(status==0)却 fp64 更差**(FD 961 棵 / 0.3%、AD 1162 棵 / 0.3%,共 340000 树)。其中:被 fast-math 真·欺骗(核自身 fp32 也 ≤ 起点)FD 367(38.2%)/ AD 785(67.6%);其余(FD 594/ AD 377)在核**自己的 fp32 目标**里也更差(accept/停止判据缺陷,非精度伪影)。严重 >2×:FD 57、AD 208。对照 Operon worse-than-start = 33。[fp32 honesty · anti-cheat] A small fraction CLAIM converged (status==0) yet are WORSE in fp64 (FD 961 / 0.3%, AD 1162 / 0.3% of 340000). Of these: genuinely fast-math-fooled (kernel's own fp32 also ≤ start) FD 367 (38.2%) / AD 785 (67.6%); the rest (FD 594 / AD 377) are worse in the kernel's OWN fp32 objective too (an accept/stop-criterion defect, not a precision artifact). Serious >2×: FD 57, AD 208. Operon worse-than-start = 33.
证据 / evidence: 中立 fp64 重算每个引擎返回系数。任意 status 的单向恶化尾(>100× 更差):FD 39、AD 199。最坏有限损失比:Operon ~3e5× vs FD ~3e22×、AD ~5e81×——核的尾比 Operon 重 ~17–76 个数量级(Operon 也会在其 33 棵更差树上放大,但远轻)。>100× 子集由 probes/probe_fp32_lie.py 复算核对。Neutral fp64 re-score. One-sided worsening tail (>100× worse, any status): FD 39, AD 199. Worst FINITE loss-ratio: Operon ~3e5× vs FD ~3e22×, AD ~5e81× — the kernel's tail is ~17–76 orders of magnitude heavier (Operon also blows up on its 33 worse trees, but far less). The >100× subset is recomputed by probes/probe_fp32_lie.py.
【失败标签校正·严抓项】核的 status==2 标为“NaN”,但其实是 λ>1e12 的阻尼停滞:FD 8028 中仅 22 棵、AD 18493 中仅 22 棵真正非有限,其余 ~99.8% 返回有限的 best-so-far。故应读作“未收敛(停滞/NaN)”,而非数值发散。[failure-label correction · anti-cheat] The kernel's status==2 is labeled 'NaN' but is really a λ>1e12 damping STALL: of FD 8028 only 22, of AD 18493 only 22 are truly non-finite; the other ~99.8% return a finite best-so-far. Read it as 'failed-to-converge (stall/NaN)', not numerical divergence.
证据 / evidence: status==2 且中立 fp64 损失非有限 才是真 NaN;其余为 best-so-far 有限值(batch_lm_*.cu: λ>1e12→finished=2)。status==2 AND non-finite neutral fp64 loss = true NaN; the rest return finite best-so-far (batch_lm_*.cu: λ>1e12 → finished=2).
【外部标尺】在双方都显著改进的子集上,质量与 Operon 互有胜负(中位接近打平),核劣化尾更长。注:该子集**剔除了**单向救援树(核改进而 Operon 未改进者),故不含正是头条稳健性信号的 AD 救援。[yardstick] On trees both genuinely improved, quality vs Operon is mixed (near-tie median); the kernel has a longer degradation tail. Note: this set EXCLUDES one-sided rescues (kernel improved but Operon did not), so it cannot contain the very AD rescues that are the headline robustness signal.
证据 / evidence: FD/Operon: 中位 log10 0.0, p90 2.4104(n=255068;另剔除 Operon-only 10521、核-only 62055);AD/Operon: 中位 0.0, p90 2.3139(n=262239)。log10>0=核损失更高。FD/Operon: median log10 0.0, p90 2.4104 (n=255068; dropped Operon-only 10521, kernel-only 62055); AD/Operon: median 0.0, p90 2.3139 (n=262239). log10>0 = kernel loss higher.

注意事项 / Caveats

  • 核跑在 **Operon 分布**的树上(每叶一个系数,K≈叶子数,比 evogp 密 ~2.8×)—— 对核是压力测试,非其目标工况。如实披露。The kernel runs on the OPERON distribution (every leaf a coefficient, K≈#leaves, ~2.8× denser than evogp) — a STRESS test for the kernel, not its design workload.
  • **核 vs Operon 是外部标尺,非受控实验**:核是 fp32+fast-math+简单 λ-damping,Operon 用非 fast-math 求值 + fp64 线性求解 + 成熟 trust-region,Jacobian 也不同 —— 差异无法归因到单一因素(精度差距勿过度解读:Operon 并非全程 fp64)。**AD vs FD 才是受控对比**(同 fp32 LM,仅 Jacobian 不同),可归因。kernel-vs-Operon is an EXTERNAL YARDSTICK, not controlled: fp32+fast-math+simple λ-damping vs Operon's non-fast-math eval + fp64 linear solve + mature trust-region, different Jacobian — gap not attributable to one factor (don't over-read the precision gap: Operon is not end-to-end fp64). Only AD-vs-FD is controlled (same fp32 LM, only the Jacobian differs).
  • “收敛率”不可跨引擎直比:Operon 的 Success 与核的 status==0 判据不同。正文用中立的损失比分布(loss_final/loss_start)与 >1% 显著改进率作同口径指标。'Convergence rate' is not cross-engine comparable (Operon Success vs kernel status==0 differ). The apples-to-apples metrics are the neutral loss-ratio distribution and the >1% meaningful-improvement rate.
  • 收敛上限:Operon 在 500 内收敛(最大 498,0.08% 接近上限);核用 1000(给足,避免低估核)。即便如此,个别 nguyen 高代 cell 仍有最多 11.1% 的核树触顶——这些是振荡/慢收敛树。中立 achieved-loss 头条指标对残留 maxiter 稳健(触顶树仍贡献其 best-so-far 损失);受影响的是核“收敛率”诊断,非损失比。Convergence caps: Operon converges within 500 (max 498, 0.08% near cap); the kernel gets 1000 (generous, to avoid understating it). Even so, a few high-gen nguyen cells leave up to 11.1% of kernel trees at maxiter — oscillating/slow-converging trees. The neutral achieved-loss headline is robust to residual maxiter (those trees still contribute their best-so-far loss); only the kernel 'convergence rate' diagnostic is affected.
  • 中立评分用精确 fp64;核用 fast-math fp32 —— 在奇点附近二者可差 10^数十×(核的 fast-math 倒数 vs 精确 fp64)。质量比较仅在双方都显著改进的子集上,并单独报告 converged-but-worsened 率。Neutral scoring is exact fp64; the kernel uses fast-math fp32 — near singularities the two can differ by 10^tens× (fast-math reciprocal vs exact fp64). Quality is compared only where both genuinely improved; the converged-but-worsened rate is reported separately.
