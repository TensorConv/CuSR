CuSR baseline: Operon LMOptimizer vs the GPU kernel (FD & AD) on identical pre-CO trees

2026-06-17

问题 / Question. 在完全相同的 pre-CO 树上,我们的 GPU 常数优化核(FD 与 AD 两版)相比成熟 CPU 优化器 Operon LMOptimizer,收敛性与最终拟合质量如何?全部 17 题,看能发现什么。On byte-identical pre-CO trees, how do our GPU constant-optimization kernels (FD and AD) compare to a mature CPU optimizer (Operon's LMOptimizer) in convergence and final fit quality? All 17 problems — exploratory.

实验设置 / Setup. 每题(cap=32, seed=0, noise=0)用 operon_dump.run_cell 确定性重生成 Operon 语料中完全相同的 pre-CO 种群(逐字节 == 已提交语料,Probe B)。每个 checkpoint 代:(1) Operon 自带 CoefficientOptimizer(LMOptimizer) 从相同 c_init 优化每棵树;(2) 核 FD(batch_lm_fusedfd)与 AD(batch_lm_ad)跑同一批 pop.bin。三者同起点(Probe A:相同起点/相同目标 0.5·SSE/相同 K/确定性);最终用中立 fp64 0.5·Σ(f-y)² 对每个引擎返回的系数统一重算 —— 与引擎自报代价无关。Per problem (cap=32, seed=0, noise=0) we deterministically regenerate the EXACT Operon pre-CO population via operon_dump.run_cell (byte-identical to the committed corpus — Probe B). Per checkpoint gen: (1) Operon's own CoefficientOptimizer(LMOptimizer) optimizes every tree from the harvested c_init; (2) kernel FD (batch_lm_fusedfd) and AD (batch_lm_ad) run on the same pop.bin. All three start from the same c_init (Probe A: same start / same 0.5·SSE objective / same K / deterministic). Each engine's returned coefficients are scored by the NEUTRAL fp64 0.5·Σ(f-y)².

发现 / Findings

【受控·主结果】AD 比 FD 更稳健:全代池合计 46574 棵 FD-Cholesky-失败 在 AD 下转为收敛(收敛 AD 283997 vs FD 241653 / 340000 可优化树;Cholesky AD 32036 vs FD 77517)。代价:AD 新增 8069 棵 0→4、6214 棵 0→2,NaN 总数 AD 18493 > FD 8028。[CONTROLLED · headline] AD is more robust than FD: 46574 FD-Cholesky-fails become converged under AD (converged AD 283997 vs FD 241653 of 340000; Cholesky AD 32036 vs FD 77517). Cost: AD adds 8069 (0→4) and 6214 (0→2); NaN total AD 18493 > FD 8028.
证据 / evidence: AD-vs-FD 状态迁移(同 fp32 LM,仅 Jacobian 不同):4→0=46574, 4→4=22894, 0→4=8069, 0→2=6214。AD-vs-FD status migration (same fp32 LM, only Jacobian differs): 4→0=46574, 4→4=22894, 0→4=8069, 0→2=6214.
【受控】无导数 bug:FD 与 AD 都显著改进的 312382 棵树上,最终 0.5·SSE 几乎一致(log10(AD/FD) 中位 -0.0;AD 更优 71097, FD 更优 72615)。[CONTROLLED] No derivative bug: on 312382 trees where both FD and AD genuinely improve, final 0.5·SSE is essentially identical (median log10(AD/FD)=-0.0; AD-better 71097, FD-better 72615).
证据 / evidence: 共同显著改进集质量比 p10 -0.4305, p90 0.3395。common-genuinely-improved quality ratio p10 -0.4305, p90 0.3395.
【同口径效能】用中立损失比(loss_final/loss_start)衡量,核与 Operon 量级相当 —— 而非二元 improved% 给出的虚高印象(核几乎每棵都微调下降)。[apples-to-apples efficacy] By the neutral loss-ratio (loss_final/loss_start), the kernel and Operon are of comparable magnitude — unlike the inflated picture from binary improved% (the kernel nudges almost every tree down a hair).
证据 / evidence: 各代中位损失比 — Operon: g0: 0.4465; g4: 0.0671; g16: 0.0016; g64: 0.0014; g100: 0.0035; FD: g0: 0.4142; g4: 0.081; g16: 0.003; g64: 0.0063; g100: 0.0097; AD: g0: 0.4046; g4: 0.0801; g16: 0.0031; g64: 0.0043; g100: 0.0062（越低越好,<1=改进）。median loss-ratio by gen — Operon: g0: 0.4465; g4: 0.0671; g16: 0.0016; g64: 0.0014; g100: 0.0035; FD: g0: 0.4142; g4: 0.081; g16: 0.003; g64: 0.0063; g100: 0.0097; AD: g0: 0.4046; g4: 0.0801; g16: 0.0031; g64: 0.0043; g100: 0.0062 (lower is better, <1 = improved).
【外部标尺】对成熟 fp64 LM 的稳健性差距随 bloat 增大,AD 缩小但未消除。[yardstick] The robustness gap to a mature fp64 LM widens with bloat; AD shrinks but does not close it.
证据 / evidence: Operon 显著改进(>1%)的树中,核未能显著改进的比例 — g0: FD 1.8% / AD 1.0%; g4: FD 1.2% / AD 0.5%; g16: FD 2.3% / AD 1.2%; g64: FD 6.3% / AD 1.6%; g100: FD 7.0% / AD 1.9%Of trees Operon improved >1%, fraction where the kernel did NOT improve >1% — g0: FD 1.8% / AD 1.0%; g4: FD 1.2% / AD 0.5%; g16: FD 2.3% / AD 1.2%; g64: FD 6.3% / AD 1.6%; g100: FD 7.0% / AD 1.9%
【fp32 诚实性·严抓项】核以 fast-math fp32 优化,少数树**自报收敛(status==0)却 fp64 更差**(占全部 340000 可优化树实例:FD 0.3%、AD 0.3%):FD 961 棵(其中真·fp32 谎报 367、严重 >2× 57),AD 1162 棵(谎报 785、严重 >2× 208)。AD 因精确导数更易冲进 fp32 奇点,严重案例约为 FD 的 3.6×。对照 Operon worse-than-start = 33(同样非零,但少 1–2 个数量级)。[fp32 honesty · anti-cheat] The kernel optimizes a fast-math fp32 proxy; a small fraction of the 340000 optimizable tree-instances (FD 0.3%, AD 0.3%) CLAIM converged (status==0) yet are WORSE in fp64: FD 961 (genuine fp32-lies 367, serious >2× 57), AD 1162 (lies 785, serious >2× 208). AD's exact derivatives drive harder into fp32 singularities — ~3.6× the serious cases of FD. Operon worse-than-start = 33 (also nonzero, 1–2 orders fewer).
证据 / evidence: 中立 fp64 重算每个引擎返回系数;status==0 且 fp64 损失 > 起点 即 converged-but-worsened;核自身 fp32 损失 ≤ 起点 的子集 = 真·fp32 谎报(被 fast-math 欺骗,已抽查 >100× 子集证实);>2× 子集 = 不可能是 fp32 噪声的严重案例。Neutral fp64 re-score of every engine's coefficients; status==0 with fp64 loss > start = converged-but-worsened; the subset whose kernel fp32 loss ≤ start = genuine fp32-lie (verified on the >100× subset); the >2× subset = serious cases that cannot be fp32 noise.
【外部标尺】在双方都显著改进的子集上,质量与 Operon 互有胜负(中位接近打平),核劣化尾更长。[yardstick] On trees both genuinely improved, quality vs Operon is mixed (near-tie median); the kernel has a longer degradation tail.
证据 / evidence: FD/Operon: 中位 log10 0.0, p90 2.4104(n=255068); AD/Operon: 中位 0.0, p10 -0.9003, p90 2.3139(n=262239)。log10>0=核损失更高。FD/Operon: median log10 0.0, p90 2.4104 (n=255068); AD/Operon: median 0.0, p10 -0.9003, p90 2.3139 (n=262239). log10>0 = kernel loss higher.

注意事项 / Caveats

  • 核跑在 **Operon 分布**的树上(每叶一个系数,K≈叶子数,比 evogp 密 ~2.8×)—— 对核是压力测试,非其目标工况。如实披露。The kernel runs on the OPERON distribution (every leaf a coefficient, K≈#leaves, ~2.8× denser than evogp) — a STRESS test for the kernel, not its design workload.
  • **核 vs Operon 是外部标尺,非受控实验**:核是 fp32+fast-math+简单 λ-damping,Operon 是 fp64+成熟 trust-region,Jacobian 也不同 —— 差异无法归因到单一因素。**AD vs FD 才是受控对比**(同 fp32 LM,仅 Jacobian 不同),可归因。kernel-vs-Operon is an EXTERNAL YARDSTICK, not controlled: fp32+fast-math+simple λ-damping vs fp64+mature trust-region, different Jacobian — gap not attributable to one factor. Only AD-vs-FD is controlled (same fp32 LM, only the Jacobian differs).
  • “收敛率”不可跨引擎直比:Operon 的 Success 与核的 status==0 判据不同。正文用中立的损失比分布(loss_final/loss_start)与 >1% 显著改进率作同口径指标。'Convergence rate' is not cross-engine comparable (Operon Success vs kernel status==0 differ). The apples-to-apples metrics are the neutral loss-ratio distribution and the >1% meaningful-improvement rate.
  • 收敛上限:Operon 在 500 内收敛(最大 498,0.08% 接近上限);核用 1000(给足,避免低估核)。即便如此,个别 nguyen 高代 cell 仍有最多 11.1% 的核树触顶——这些是振荡/慢收敛树。中立 achieved-loss 头条指标对残留 maxiter 稳健(触顶树仍贡献其 best-so-far 损失);受影响的是核“收敛率”诊断,非损失比。Convergence caps: Operon converges within 500 (max 498, 0.08% near cap); the kernel gets 1000 (generous, to avoid understating it). Even so, a few high-gen nguyen cells leave up to 11.1% of kernel trees at maxiter — oscillating/slow-converging trees. The neutral achieved-loss headline is robust to residual maxiter (those trees still contribute their best-so-far loss); only the kernel 'convergence rate' diagnostic is affected.
  • 中立评分用精确 fp64;核用 fast-math fp32 —— 在奇点附近二者可差 10^数十×(核的 fast-math 倒数 vs 精确 fp64)。质量比较仅在双方都显著改进的子集上,并单独报告 converged-but-worsened 率。Neutral scoring is exact fp64; the kernel uses fast-math fp32 — near singularities the two can differ by 10^tens× (fast-math reciprocal vs exact fp64). Quality is compared only where both genuinely improved; the converged-but-worsened rate is reported separately.
