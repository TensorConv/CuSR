- Operon reuse is disclosed, but the sentinel file itself says `overall: STOP` with worst drift `43.3%` in [operon_sentinels.json](/home/weish/hao/CuSR/experiments/e7_section2/out/operon_sentinels.json:2). I would allow the crossover only as approximate/order-of-magnitude, not as precise `3.6x` style claims without that caveat.
- The large speedups use core/in-loop throughput. Recomputed e2e speedups are much lower: revad vs 128c e2e median about `1.17x`, max `14.93x`; revad vs 1c e2e median about `7.96x`, max `50.42x`.
- “large-M cells” is loose for the `120x` max: that best cell is `M=16000,N=1000`, not the largest M. The `28x` vs 128c max is `M=64000,N=100`.
